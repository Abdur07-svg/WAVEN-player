WAVEN Privacy Policy

WAVEN is designed from the ground up as a local, offline music player for Android. Your personal music collection and listening habits remain completely on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

WAVEN does not require user accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising SDKs, telemetry, or third-party behavioral analytics. Your local audio files, playlists, and listening history stay exclusively on your Android device.

2. Android App Permissions

  • Audio / Media Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used strictly on your device to discover and play local music files you store on your phone.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Used solely to continue playback in the background and provide lock-screen, notification, and Bluetooth media controls.

3. Official Website & APK Downloads

This website is hosted statically on GitHub Pages to showcase the WAVEN Android application and connect visitors directly to official APK releases on GitHub. Your chosen Dark/Light website theme preference is stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

4. Repository & Releases

Review release notes and project updates in the official WAVEN GitHub repository.
